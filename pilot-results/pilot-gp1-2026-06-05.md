## Analysis

### Core finding: review volume determines recoverability

| App | Daily review rate | Reviews needed to reach Nov 2025 | Feasible? |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | ~3,300/day | ~2.3 million | ❌ No |
| Grammarly | ~400/month | ~**2,500** | ✅ Yes |
| Spotify | ~770/day | ~260,000 | ❌ No |

### How many reviews to reach 2021 for Grammarly?

- Jun 2026 → Jan 2021 = approximately 65 months
- Rate: ~400 reviews/month
- Estimated total needed: ~**26,000 reviews**
- Conclusion: **fully feasible once the 10,000-review cap is removed** — `google-play-scraper` has no hard pagination limit; continuation tokens work indefinitely

### Google Play vs. Apple App Store comparison

| | Apple App Store RSS | Google Play (google-play-scraper) |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum retrievable reviews | **50** (pagination broken) | **No hard limit** (continuation token) |
| Grammarly: can reach Nov 2025? | ❌ Earliest: 2026-05-20 | ✅ ~2,500 reviews sufficient |
| ChatGPT: can reach Nov 2025? | ❌ | ❌ (volume too high) |

---

## Verdict

**Medium-volume apps (e.g., Grammarly): ✅ GREEN**
Google Play reviews can reach the Nov 2025 policy window with ~2,500 reviews; reaching back to 2021 requires ~26,000 reviews — technically straightforward with no API barrier.

**Very high-volume apps (e.g., ChatGPT, Spotify): ❌ RED**
Review generation rate is too high; reaching 2021–2025 would require millions of reviews, making it impractical.

**Research design implication:**
Any Google Play-based sample must stratify apps by review volume. High-volume apps (ChatGPT, Spotify) are not recoverable to the 2021–2025 window. The viable sample consists of **medium-volume AI apps and matched controls** — not the largest apps on the platform.
